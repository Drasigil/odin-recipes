Recipe

Ingredients Needed

For the Pasta Dough

  • All-purpose flour
    • Optional: Combined with semolina flour
  • Eggs

For the Cheese

  • Ricotta cheese
  • Smoked gruyere
  • Burrata
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Black pepper (0.5-1 tsp)

Optional Extras

  • Chopped rosemary or thyme
  • Lemon juice (1 tsp) and zest

Steps

  1. Make the filling for the raviolis
    1. Combine all ingredients in a food processor
    2. Place mixture into a piping bag or airtight container
    3. Refrigerate for 1 hour
  2. Make homemade pasta dough
  3. Shape the ravioli
    1. Pipe or spoon generous tbsps of the cheese mixture against center line of dough sheet
    2. Fold the dough sheet over; keep it lifted with your left hand and use right hand to push air out of ravioli
    3. Press firmly to adhere folded dough sheet to itself
    4. Trim the edges with a ravioli cutter, pizza, pastry cutter, or knife
      • Note: Reroll any offcuts
  4. Place raviolis on a lined tray in a single layer, covered loosely with plastic wrap
  5. Later boil in well-salted water for 4-5 minutes, then add favorite sauce
