Description

An Italian pasta dish comprised of long, flat noodles. It can be served many ways, but its most iconic form is the stacked and layered dish of pasta and ground meat, baked and covered in sauce.

History of the dish

A dish that originated in Italy during the middle ages, considered relatively expensive at the time of its origin. The first recorded recipe was in the early 14th century, which features fermented dough flattened into thin sheets, which were then broiled and sprinkled with cheese and spices. This version of the dish was eaten with a small, pointed stick.

Interesting tidbit

The oldest known written reference to this dish appeared in 1282, as part of a ballad that was transcribed by a Bolognese notary: Pur bii del vin, comadre, e no lo temperare, which translates to "Just drink some wine, my woman, and do not dilute it."

Recipe

Ingredients Needed

  • Cheese filling containing ricotta and parmesan, with seasonings
  • Meat: Italian sausage and ground beef
  • Sauce: Rao's marinara sauce
  • Optional: Spinach (10oz)

Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in a large pot of salted water
  2. Prepare the meat sauce
    1. Cook the sausage and beef with onion and garlic
    2. Drain water, add pasta sauce, and then simmer for a few minutes until thickened
  3. Combine the cheese mixture by mixing together in a bowl
  4. Layer and bake the lasagna
    1. Spread 1 cup of meat sauce into a 9"x13" pan
    2. Add a layer of noodles
    3. Top the noodles with the cheese mixture
    4. Add another layer of meat sauce
    5. Add another layer of noodles
    6. Add another layer of cheese mixture
    7. Repeat the layers and end with a layer of noodles and sauce
    8. Cover with foil and bake
    9. Top with mozzarella and parmesan after removing the foil, then bake for 15 more minutes
  5. Let the lasagna rest for 15-45 minutes after baking
